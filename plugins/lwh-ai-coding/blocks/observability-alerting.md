# Observability · alerting

### Upstream probes
- What: Watch the services you depend on.
- Applies when: the app is live and depends on outside services.
- Stage: with-users
- Kind: checkable

### Actionable alerts
- What: Page on symptoms, not noise.
- Applies when: the app is live and sends alerts.
- Stage: with-users
- Kind: practice

### On-call and paging
- What: Someone always answers.
- Applies when: a team keeps the app running.
- Stage: at-scale
- Kind: practice

### Runbooks
- What: A checklist for 3am.
- Applies when: the app is live and things have gone wrong before.
- Stage: with-users
- Kind: practice
